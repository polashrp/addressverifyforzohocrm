Last Updated: August 2026

Address Verify & Customer Timezone for Zoho CRM ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Zoho CRM extension.

Information We Collect

1. Information You Provide

When you install and use our extension, we may collect:

  • CRM Data: Address information, contact details, and other data you choose to process through our extension.
  • Account Information: Your Zoho CRM organization ID and user email for authentication and support purposes.

2. Information Automatically Collected

  • Usage Data: How you interact with our extension, including feature usage and verification requests.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, and device information for analytics and troubleshooting.

How We Use Your Information

  • To Provide Services: Process address verification, enrichment, and time zone lookup requests.
  • To Improve Our Extension: Analyze usage patterns to enhance features and performance.

Data Storage and Security

We do not permanently store your CRM data. Address verification requests are processed in real-time and not retained beyond what is necessary to complete the verification. All data transmitted between your Zoho CRM and our service is encrypted using industry-standard protocols.

Our extension may use third-party address verification services to process your requests. These providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and use it solely for the purpose of providing the verification service.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights including:

  • Accessing the data we hold about you.

  • Withdrawing consent at any time.
  • Objecting to certain data processing activities.

Data Retention

We retain usage logs and analytics data for a limited period to improve our service. These logs do not contain personally identifiable information beyond what is necessary for system operation.

Children's Privacy

Our extension is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
